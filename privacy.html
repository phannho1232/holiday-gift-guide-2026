Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

How we protect your data, verify merchant policies, and maintain reader trust.

1. Information We Collect

The Curated Living does not sell products directly or collect payment card details. When you browse our guides, we may collect anonymous analytics data (such as page views, scroll depth, and outbound link clicks) and standard campaign attribution parameters (such as UTM tags or click IDs) stored temporarily in your browser's session storage to measure editorial performance.

2. Affiliate Links & Third-Party Merchants

When you click "View at Brand" or similar outbound buttons on our site, you are directed to the official online store of that brand (such as Cozy Earth, Boarderie, StoryWorth, REVOMADIC, Tinggly, BLUETTI, or JMGO). Any purchase you complete is governed by the privacy policy, shipping terms, and return guarantee of that respective merchant.

3. Shipping Cutoff & Return Verification Policy

Our editorial team verifies each merchant's stated holiday shipping cutoffs and return windows as of November 2026. Because carrier capacity fluctuates in mid-December, our site automatically prioritizes instant digital eVouchers (such as Tinggly and StoryWorth) after December 14 to help readers avoid late holiday deliveries.

4. Cookies & Opt-Out

You may clear or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Our site functions normally without third-party tracking cookies enabled.

5. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, editorial corrections, or partnership questions, contact our team at hello@thecuratedliving.com.

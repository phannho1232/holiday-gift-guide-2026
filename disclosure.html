Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency is the foundation of everything we publish.

How We Earn Revenue

The Curated Living is a participant in various affiliate marketing programs. This means that when you click on certain links on our site and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission from the retailer — at absolutely no additional cost to you. These commissions help us fund our operations, pay our editors, and continue providing free, independent product reviews and gift guides.

Editorial Independence

Affiliate commissions do not influence our editorial choices. Our editors independently select, test, and review every product featured on this site. No brand, advertiser, or affiliate partner has any say in what we recommend, how we score a product, or where it appears in our rankings. If a product doesn't meet our standards, it won't be featured — regardless of the commission it might earn us.

Our Testing Standards

All products featured on The Curated Living are independently tested by our editorial team. We purchase most products ourselves; occasionally we accept review samples, but this is always disclosed and never influences our rating. Our reviews are based on real-world, hands-on testing across our four scoring criteria: wow factor, value for money, shipping & packaging, and returns & customer service.

FTC Compliance

In accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255), we disclose all material connections between The Curated Living and the products or services we review. When you see a link to a product on our site, you should assume it may be an affiliate link. We are committed to full transparency and compliance with all applicable advertising and disclosure regulations.

Questions?

If you have any questions about our affiliate relationships, editorial process, or this disclosure, please don't hesitate to reach out. You can contact us at hello@thecuratedliving.com. We're always happy to explain how we work.

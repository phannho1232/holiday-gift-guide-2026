Who We Are

The Curated Living is an independent editorial team dedicated to helping you find products that are genuinely worth your money. We are not influenced by brands, PR samples, or advertising revenue. Every product we recommend has been tested hands-on by our editors — in our homes, on our travels, and in our daily routines — so our reviews reflect real-world experience, not marketing copy.

Our Testing Process

We evaluate every product against four core criteria: wow factor (does it genuinely delight?), value for money (is it worth the price?), shipping & packaging (does it arrive on time and in good condition?), and returns & customer service (what happens if something goes wrong?). Each criterion is scored on a 10-point scale. Only products that earn an overall score of 8.0 or higher make it into our guides. The rest get filed under "tested and rejected" — because knowing what to avoid is just as valuable as knowing what to buy.

Editorial Independence

Every product featured on The Curated Living is chosen on its own merit. While we do earn a commission when you purchase through our affiliate links, this revenue never influences which products we select, how we rank them, or what we write about them. Our editorial decisions are made independently of our business relationships. If a product isn't good enough, it doesn't make the cut — regardless of the commission rate.
